HabitVault — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

HabitVault ("the app") is a habit-tracking application. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information on Android and iOS. The short version: your habit data stays local unless you choose to share it or your operating system includes it in a device backup.

Data we collect

We do not automatically collect or receive your habit data or usage data. The app has no user accounts, no sign-up, no advertising, no third-party trackers, and no analytics. Exporting a file, contacting support, and system backups are explained below.

Where your data lives

All data you create — your habits, daily check-ins, streaks, settings — is stored on your device, in the app's local database and local settings storage. We do not operate servers that receive this data. HabitVault does not provide its own cloud sync service.

Your operating system may include HabitVault's local data in your device backups. On Android, this may include backups to your Google account; on iOS, this may include iCloud Backup or a backup made to your computer. These backups are managed by your operating system and your accounts, not by us. We do not have access to them. You can manage available backup options in your device settings.

Network access

HabitVault's core features work offline. On Android, the app does not request the Internet permission. Links, email, and file sharing may open other apps or system services when you choose to use them.

Permissions and local reminders

On Android, HabitVault uses the following permissions for features on your own device:

  • VIBRATE — haptic feedback when you interact with the interface.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — optional local reminders.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — lets the app put your reminder back in place after the device restarts or the app updates.

On iOS, HabitVault asks for notification permission when you enable reminders. Reminders are off by default, and you choose their time. They are scheduled and displayed locally; no remote push service is used.

Reminder text may include habit names, the number of habits not yet marked, or the date of your last activity. Some reminders help you return after a period without marks. Notifications may appear on your lock screen according to your device's notification and preview settings. You can turn reminders off in HabitVault or manage notification permissions and previews in system settings.

Export and import

You can export your data to a JSON file and import it back. This happens only when you choose to do so. Exported files are handled by your device's own sharing and file system — the app does not send them anywhere on its own.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. HabitVault does not automatically collect habit data or usage data from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and the information you include in your message so we can respond. The app does not automatically attach your habit database or export file. Please include only the information you want to share.
