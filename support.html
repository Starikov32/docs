Frequently asked questions

Does HabitVault need an account or internet?

No. The app works fully offline, needs no account and no sign-up. All of your data stays on your device.

How do I back up or move my data to a new phone?

Open Settings → Export data to save everything as a single file. On the other device, use Settings → Import data and pick that file to restore your habits and history.

How are streaks calculated?

Missing a scheduled day breaks the streak. Days a habit isn't scheduled for are never counted against you, so a Mon/Wed/Fri habit keeps its streak over the weekend.

Can I track a habit only on certain weekdays?

Yes. A habit can run every day or on the specific weekdays you choose — for example, the gym on Monday, Wednesday and Friday.

What's the difference between a good and a bad habit?

Good habits are ones you want to build (read, work out, drink water). Bad habits are ones you want to break (no smoking, no late-night snacks). Both count toward your progress and goals.

I have a bug report or a feature request.

Please email us with as much detail as you can — what you did, what you expected and what happened. Your app version is shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.
